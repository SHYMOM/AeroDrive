Zero-Knowledge Pipeline

How AeroDrive Protects Every Byte

Click through the 5 cryptographic lifecycle steps that happen before any byte touches the network.

Step 1

Local File Chunking

Files are split locally into optimized 50MB chunks in RAM. Original file names and MIME types are isolated.

Step 2

Argon2id & HKDF Derivation

A unique ephemeral initialization vector and chunk encryption key are derived from your master vault seed.

Step 3

AES-256-CTR / GCM Encrypt

Each chunk is encrypted client-side. The resulting payload is completely indistinguishable from random noise.

Step 4

3-Channel Striping

Encrypted slices are dispatched across separate Telegram storage channels in parallel streams.

Step 5

On-Demand Sub-Chunk Stream

When playing or downloading, the app requests only the 2MB sub-range needed, decrypting directly in memory.

Under The Hood

Built for Performance & Privacy

Engineered with Flutter, C++ TDLib FFI, Go, and SQLite for cross-platform speed and zero dependencies.

⚡

C++ TDLib FFI Core

Direct native bindings to Telegram's official TDLib library with custom InputFile wrappers, ensuring ultra-reliable downloads and uploads.

Hardware Keystore / Keyring
